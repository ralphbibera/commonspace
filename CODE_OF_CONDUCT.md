# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make space for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or an appropriate private repository channel.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and apply proportionate consequences.
